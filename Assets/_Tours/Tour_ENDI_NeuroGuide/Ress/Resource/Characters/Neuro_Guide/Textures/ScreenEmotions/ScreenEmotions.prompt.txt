Use case: stylized-concept

Create one perfectly square texture atlas, exact regular 4 by 4 grid of 16 equal square cells, edge-to-edge. All cells have identical solid very dark navy almost black backgrounds. Consistent graphic language, stroke weight, face scale and eye positions throughout. Flat front-facing 2D emissive screen graphics, clean rounded shapes, extremely subtle glow only. No robot bodies, no screen outlines, no borders, no visible grid lines, no captions, no text, no watermarks, no perspective, no reflections. Each face centered precisely within its cell and confined to central 65 percent of cell, generous dark padding for UV sampling.
Exactly these expressions in row-major order from top left:
